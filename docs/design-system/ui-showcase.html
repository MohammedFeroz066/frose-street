Tactile Touch Architecture

Button Sizing & Physical Ergonomics

Street food stalls operate in high-friction physical environments: cooks have flour or oil on their hands, cashiers are multitasking between counting change and greeting customers, and bright direct sunlight washes out standard subtle mobile UIs.

84px
Extreme Height Touch Target
Standard web buttons are 40px–48px. FROSE Street uses a mandatory 84px vertical touch surface with 16px vertical padding, ensuring 100% blind-tap accuracy without looking down.
4px 3D
4px Mechanical Bottom Border & Instant Feedback
Embossed 4px solid colored base border (`border-b-4`). When tapped, translates down 3px with haptic vibration, giving instant tactile feedback even through greasy thumbs.
3 Colors
Role-Differentiated Chromatic Identification
Amber (Billing) vs Emerald (Packaging) vs Flame (Kitchen). Staff can distinguish roles across the cart from 10 feet away.
Natural Thumb Zone Compliant
Buttons positioned in the lower 60% of the screen for natural single-handed thumb operation.

Zero-Keyboard Friction

Credentials & Authentication Modes

Street vendors cannot tolerate full keyboard popups that obstruct half the screen when punching orders or logging in every morning. FROSE Street implements 3 fast-entry mechanisms:

🔑 4-Digit Quick PIN Keypad
1 Second Entry

Large 64px physical buttons on-screen. Never triggers mobile OS software keyboard. Salted scrypt verification with automatic daily lockout protection after 5 consecutive incorrect attempts.

👆 1-Tap Fingerprint / Face Unlock (WebAuthn)
Instant Touch

Leverages phone hardware security chip (FIDO2 / WebAuthn). The stall owner touches their phone's physical fingerprint reader to authenticate instantly without typing any characters.

📷 QR Scan for Cook & Packer (Zero Typing)
5-Min Single Use

Helpers scan a 5-minute single-use QR from the owner's billing phone camera. Issues role-scoped tokens directly without exposing the master password or owner PIN.

30-Second Stall Onboarding
Phone Number + Stall Name + Business Category (Momo, Chai, Shawarma, Dosa)
